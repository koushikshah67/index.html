Method: Debit/Credit amounts in the bank ledger are treated as GST-inclusive at 18%. Ex-GST amount = amount ÷ 1.18. Brokerage is computed separately on each leg: debit-side brokerage = +(ex-GST debit × brokerage %); credit-side brokerage = −(ex-GST credit × brokerage %), shown in red. The two are summed per account/broker, so credit transactions reduce net brokerage payable.

Of ledger entries, matched a "Name Of the Account" entry in Master.xlsx and are included below; the remainder (staff payouts, bank sweeps, interest, expenses, etc.) fall outside the broker book and are excluded.
